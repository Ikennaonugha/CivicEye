About CivicEye

Empowering Citizens Through Transparent Public Procurement.

CivicEye bridges the gap between official public procurement records and real-world execution across Lagos State. By ingesting open procurement data (OCDS), we make public contract details, allocated budgets, and assigned contractors fully accessible to every resident.

Through community-driven auditing, citizens can verify project sites, submit civic flags with photo evidence and GPS tags, and hold procuring entities and contractors accountable for delayed or substandard public infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about public project tracking, civic flagging, or data transparency on CivicEye? Find answers below.

CivicEye is a civic technology platform designed to foster transparency, accountability, and citizen oversight in Lagos State public procurement. It allows residents to track government-funded infrastructure projects across all 20 LGAs and report real-world execution issues.
CivicEye integrates open public procurement records structured under the Open Contracting Data Standard (OCDS), sourced from official state portals like the Lagos State Public Procurement Agency (LSPPA).
Search for a project in your Local Government Area, click "Submit Flag," and upload photo evidence from the site. CivicEye automatically captures GPS verification to validate that reports originate from actual project locations.
